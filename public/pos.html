Store Readings

Choose X Reading for a mid-shift check or Z Reading for final branch closing. Business dates use Philippine time (Asia/Manila).

X-reading — per cashier/user

View or print an in-progress cashier report. This does not close the day.

Z-reading — final daily branch close

After closing, sales, refunds, and voids for this branch are blocked until the next Philippine business day. This close cannot be undone.
